# hecate for maintainers

This site is for the people who work on hecate itself. The documentation for people who build applications with hecate
is the [user site](../../user/en/).

- [Architecture and layers](explanation/architecture-and-layers.md): how the packages are divided, how a request travels
  through them and what happens at boot.
- [Measure performance](how-to/measure-performance.md): how the benchmarks are run and read.
- [Architecture specifications](architecture/index.md): the specification of each area, generated from `docs/architecture/`.
- [Architecture decision records](adr/index.md): the decisions that bind the project, generated from `docs/adr/`.
- [Roadmap](roadmap.md): what is built, in which order, and the exit criteria of each phase, generated from `docs/roadmap.md`.
- [Open questions](open-questions.md): the decisions, research and known limitations waiting for an answer, generated from `docs/open-questions.md`.
- [Reviews](reviews/index.md): the reports of the phase gates and of the package reviews, generated from `docs/reviews/`.
